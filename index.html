snoozy

wake up calm. scroll later.

When the alarm rings, Snoozy locks your distracting apps for the quiet time you choose. Start the day with a clear head, not a feed.

set up

  • Drag the dial to set your bedtime and wake time.
  • Choose the days the alarm repeats and the alarm sound.
  • Tap "quiet list" and choose the apps, categories, and websites to lock.
  • Set quiet time from 15 to 120 minutes.
  • Turn the alarm on.

during quiet time

  • The apps on your quiet list stay locked until quiet time ends.
  • The top of the app shows how much quiet time is left.
  • You cannot turn off the alarm until quiet time ends.
  • Snooze delays the alarm sound. Quiet time still starts at the original alarm time.

common questions

My apps are still locked after quiet time. Open Snoozy, turn the alarm off, and turn it on again.

Snoozy cannot lock apps. Open Snoozy and tap "quiet list". If Snoozy asks for Screen Time access, allow it. Then choose your apps again.
