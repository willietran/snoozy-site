snoozy

privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Snoozy does not collect any data about you.

what snoozy does not do

  • Snoozy has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Snoozy does not send any data to a server. The app makes no network requests.
  • Snoozy has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Snoozy does not sell or share data with anyone.

what stays on your iphone

Snoozy saves your alarm settings on your iPhone: the wake time, the bedtime, the repeat days, the alarm sound, and the length of quiet time. Snoozy also saves your quiet list. Snoozy keeps these settings in storage that only Snoozy and its own Screen Time extension can read. If you delete Snoozy, your iPhone deletes these settings.

screen time

Snoozy uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock the apps on your quiet list. When you choose apps, categories, or websites, Apple gives Snoozy a coded reference for each one. Snoozy cannot see the names of the apps or websites you choose, and it cannot see how you use them. Snoozy uses these references only to lock your choices during quiet time.

alarms

Snoozy uses Apple's AlarmKit framework to schedule your alarm on your iPhone. The alarm schedule does not leave your iPhone.

children

Snoozy does not collect information from anyone, including children.

changes
